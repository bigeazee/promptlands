How it works

  1. Rank yours. One comparison at a time. Around four or five per item, so thirty items takes about four minutes.

  2. Send it to one other person. You get a file at the end. They open this page, load it, and rank the same items themselves. Two people is enough.

  3. Load both files. You get only the items you ranked differently, biggest gap first. Not a combined list — the gaps are the point.

Everything happens inside this page. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored anywhere but your own browser, and there is no account. Part of PromptLands.
